                        Defense-in-Depth
                        
Architectural Checkpoints & Safeguards

                            L1
                            INGRESS NORMALIZER
                        

                        
Ingress Normalizer & De-obfuscator

                        

                            Strips adversarial obfuscation before tokenization. Translates Cyrillic homoglyphs (Іgnоrе $\rightarrow$ Ignore), removes invisible zero-width spaces, decodes Base64 payloads, URL percent codes, and Hex/Unicode escape sequences.
                        

                        

                            
• Strips: \u200B-\u200D, \uFEFF, \u00A0

                            
• Decodes: Base64, \x41, \u0041, %20

                            
• Quarantines: <|im_start|>, <system>

                        

                    

                    

                        
                    

                


                
                

                    

                        

                            L2
                            PRIVACY SHIELD
                        

                        
PII Redaction & Surrogate Tokenizer

                        

                            Safeguards customer confidentiality and compliance (GLBA, PCI-DSS, GDPR). Detects valid credit card numbers using the Luhn checksum algorithm, bank routing numbers, and SSNs. Replaces them with reversible surrogate tokens.
                        

                        

                            
• Luhn-verified Credit Cards

                            
• US Social Security Numbers

                            
• Reversible In-Context Detokenizer

                        

                    

                    

                        Automatic on all inputs
                    

                


                
                

                    

                        

                            L3
                            SEMANTIC GUARD
                        

                        
Semantic Guardrail & Risk Scoring

                        

                            Heuristic classification mapped to MITRE ATLAS. Evaluates instruction overrides, DAN roleplay personas, system prompt extraction probes, gaslighting, and Shannon entropy anomalies. Calculates composite risk scores from 0.00 to 1.00.
                        

                        

                            
• MITRE ATLAS: AML.T0051, AML.T0054

                            
• Shannon Entropy Suffix Detection

                            
• Policy Decisions: ALLOW, STEP_UP, BLOCK

                        

                    

                    

                        
                    

                


                
                

                    

                        

                            L4
                            TOOL POLICY GATE
                        

                        
Tool Execution Gate & Boundaries

                        

                            Rigid Pydantic schema validation preventing Excessive Agency. Enforces positive amounts ($amount > 0$), least-privilege account ownership, memo prompt injection sanitization, and step-up MFA threshold ceiling ($1,000).
                        

                        

                            
• Ambiguity Boundary: Amount & Recipient Required

                            
• Step-Up MFA Challenge (> $1,000)

                            
• Poisoned Memo Directive Sanitizer

                        

                    

                    

                        
                    

                


                
                

                    

                        

                            L5
                            EGRESS SANITIZER
                        

                        
Honeytoken Canary & Egress Scrub

                        

                            Injects unique cryptographic session honeytokens into downstream reasoning contexts. Egress filter monitors model responses and automatically scrubs canaries, confidential directives, and raw prompt leaks before client delivery.
                        

                        

                            
• Ephemeral Session Honeytokens

                            
• Leakage Detection & Auto-Scrubbing

                            
• System Directive Masking

                        

                    

                    

                        Active on all egress streams
